# 生图提示词模板

每张图单独生成，并把 `assets/amber-character-reference.png` 作为“角色身份参考图”。参考图只用于锁定 Amber，不用于复制版式或背景。

```text
Use case: scientific-educational
Asset type: 16:9 horizontal illustration placed inside a Chinese article
Primary request: Create one standalone conceptual illustration for {主题}.

Character reference:
Use the supplied approved Amber Q-version sheet to preserve both character and visual-system identity. Amber is consistently about 3–3.25 heads tall, with red sunglasses resting on her head, long wavy black hair with sparse warm-golden tips, a relaxed oversized powder-blue collared shirt with rolled sleeves, creamy-ivory wide-leg trousers, a tiny bright-yellow belt/scarf accent, small hoop earrings, and black-and-ivory casual shoes. Use simple dot eyes, small eyebrows and mouth, one clean hair silhouette, and restrained but distinct expressions. Do not change her into tall fashion proportions, a two-head baby mascot, a brown T-shirt character, or a sleeveless-vest character.

Core idea: {核心意思}
Scene and action: {Amber 在哪里、亲自在做什么、主要物件如何表达概念}
Short Chinese labels, verbatim: {3–6 个短词；不需要则留空}

Style:
Pure white background, minimalist black hand-drawn line art, slightly wobbly thin pen lines, lots of negative space, clean absurd product-sketch feeling. The person and every object use exactly the same line weight and simplified detail level. Use powder blue for folders, books, chair surfaces, panels and secondary information; bright yellow for handles, buttons, joints, plugs, pens and action paths; creamy ivory for quiet surfaces. Red appears only on Amber's sunglasses.

Composition:
One core conceptual action only. Main subject occupies roughly 40%–60% of the canvas; keep at least 35% clean white space. Amber performs the core action and occupies roughly 15%–30% of the image.

Constraints:
No title in the top-left. No structure-type label. No PPT infographic grid, dense explainer, commercial poster, realistic UI, beige paper, paper texture, shadows, gradients, decorative sticker sheet, or children's illustration. No orange main paths and no red accent objects. Avoid glossy anime eyes and exaggerated expressions. The idea should be understandable primarily from the action and objects, not from text.
```
